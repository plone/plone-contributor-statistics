- “Intro to CastleCMS” by Kim Nguyen is a half-day session for content editors, decision makers, site owners.

- “Mastering Plone 5 Development” by Philip Bauer and Thomas Schorr is a two-day session for developers. Learn how to develop custom websites in Plone 5 using the lates best-practices.

- “Hacking Plone” by Jean Jordaan, Mike Metcalfe, and Kim Nguyen is a one-day session for Web developers, power users and content management leads. Create custom content types, a design for a website, layouts for homepages and content types, custom application logic, and forms-with-workflow application logic. All in the browser!

---

- “Guillotina” by Ramon Navarro and Victor Fernández de Alba is a one-day session for Python and JavaScript developers. Get started with Guillotina: the asyncio REST resource API.

---

- “React” by Rob Gietema, Roel Bruggink is a one-day session for developers. Get started with React & friends.

---

- “Transmogrifier” by Chrissy Wainwright is a one-day session for developers. Learn how to migrate content (whether from Wordpress, Plone, or another CMS) into a new Plone site.

---

- “Demystifying ZODB”, by Jim Fulton is a half-day session that aims to demystify ZODB and its role in Plone, with an emphasis on hands-on exploration. We'll review core concepts and things you need to know to get the most out of your Plone ZODB databases.
