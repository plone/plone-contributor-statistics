Martin Raspe

---

Martin Raspe (PhD in Art History, Freiburg University, 1991) works as scholar and IT consultant at the "Bibliotheca Hertziana" (Max-Planck-Institute for Art History) in Rome, Italy. He is a self-taught programmer with experience in Perl, Python, ZOPE and XML processing. His main field of interest - apart from Renaissance and Baroque architecture - is the development of database infrastructure for academic research, with particular regard to the integration of images and scholarly metadata. He worked on WIRE ("Word and Image Retrieval Environment", a tool for collecting visual and textual data in the humanities), "Lineamenta", a web-based database of architectural drawings, and conceived the project ZUCCARO (Zope-based Universally Configurable Classes for Academic Research Online), which will be presented at this conference.
