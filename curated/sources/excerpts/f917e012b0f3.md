| Plone as a Development Platform Hosted by: Elizabeth Leddy Like it or not, Plone is a platform. It is a powerful starting point for building intranets, extranets, and all sorts of things that should never be built on Plone but we do it anyways. Accepting this fate, what can we do to provide a friendlier Plone for developers? This talk will address what is wrong with the API, what are we currently doing to fix it, why you should care and how you can participate whether you are a developer, integrator, or business leader. |
