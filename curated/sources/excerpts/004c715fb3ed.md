David Convent works at the Royal Belgian Institute of Natural Sciences in Brussels. He currently works as main developer of the MARS framework, a plone based application for storing all possible data related to the anthropological and archeological collections of the three project partners: The Royal Belgian Museum for Central Africa, the Royal Musums of Art and History and the Royal Belgian Institute of Natural Sciences. He is the main contributor to CMFBibliographyAT developed by Raphael Ritz, and the author of ATBiblioList (an add-on to CMFBibliographyAT). He also wrote DIYPloneStyle (which supersedes SimplePloneStyle and MySkin), a skeleton product for building new Plone 2.1 skins (in the graphical meaning). As further service to the community, he is comanager of the http://plone.be extension of the http://plone.org web site.
