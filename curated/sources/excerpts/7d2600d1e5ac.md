Introduced to Plone a long, long time ago, Kim led the development and deployment of over 350 Plone sites at the University of Wisconsin Oshkosh, and currently leads the project strategy and management team at Six Feet Up. Kim has been a member of the Plone Foundation Board since 2014 and currently serves as the Plone Communications and Marketing team leader. He was an organizer of Plone Symposium Midwest 2013, Plone Symposium Midwest 2014, Plone Conference 2016, and Plone Conference 2017.
