Since 2007 Alin has worked at Eau de Web in Romania, mainly on European Environment Agency projects designing and developing small to large-scale Plone solutions. Many of the features that were developed (EEA Faceted Navigation, EEA Daviz, etc) are available as Plone add-ons and very popular within the Plone community. Alin is also an active member of the Plone Foundation Framework Team, Installers Team and Volto Team.
