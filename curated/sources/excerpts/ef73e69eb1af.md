…The Open Planning Project (TOPP), a high tech non-profit based in New York City, is using Plone in creative ways as we fulfill our mission to build tools that enhance the role of the citizen in democracy. We draw inspiration from the ideas, processes and success of the Open Source movement, and believe that Open Source principles can be effective in tackling real world problems. As both integrators of existing software and developers of the next generation of Plone products (Wicked, Listen, Remember), we have used Plone in ways that are far beyond traditional content management.
