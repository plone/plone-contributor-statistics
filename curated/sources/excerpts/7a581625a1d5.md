Cris started in the field of computer music, and since 2001 has been building web-based applications. He has contributed to numerous open source projects including the Plone add-ons Faculty/Staff Directory, bibliograph.parsing, and collective.isotope. He is an experienced teacher and provides Plone, Django, Pyramid and Python training at all levels, including starting the Python program at Code Fellows in Seattle, WA. He was a keynote speaker at PyCon 2016, where he reintroduced Plone and Zope to the Python community. He is currently a member of the Jazkarta team.
