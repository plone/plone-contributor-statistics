Mike Halm is the Director and Senior Strategist for the WebLion group. The group started modestly over five years ago with just one developer and has now grown to 10 full-time developers.  In addition to this core group, there are over thirty partners with about 80 to 100 developers, integrators and web designers located across the Penn State system of 25 campuses in various colleges, department and administrative groups. Mike Halm was the principal catalyst for starting the WebLion group with his colleague Christian Vinten-Johansen. Mike has presented extensively on the role of standards and the use of open source at international venues over the past ten years.
