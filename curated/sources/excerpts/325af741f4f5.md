Thomas Lotze

---

Thomas has been using Python since 2004 to develop web applications for productive use. Since 2016 he has been working with Starzel. He has many years of experience with the application frameworks Zope (both 2 and 3), Plone and Pyramid. His particular interest lies with software infrastructure and automated testing. He is convinced of the ideas of Free Software and actively contributes to the development of components he uses, among them core packages like Zope itself.
