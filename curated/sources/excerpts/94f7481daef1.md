Víctor Fernández de Alba

---

Víctor has been part of the Plone community since 2006. He currently works as Senior Software Engineer at kitconcept GmbH. In the past, he worked for the Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya · BarcelonaTech for 15 years, who have over 450 Plone sites deployed. He is also co-author of the multilingual add-on and the default theme for Plone 5. He has organized several sprints and is the team leader of the new plone.org project. He's also been deeply involved in plone.restapi and helped to evolve the HeadlessCMS project in Plone.
