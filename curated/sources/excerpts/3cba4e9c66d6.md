Title: Find affiliation evidence in the GitHub commit search
Author: jensens
Date: 2026-10-20T22:55:24Z

Refs #139. The reaching-back ranges of the migration can't be trimmed yet: measured against the collected activity, a strict trim would turn 48% of all commits and pull requests Unmapped, 94% in 2024 and 2025, because the current entries, GitHub profiles read in 2026, date nothing. This pull request adds the evidence source that scales, runs it, and applies the result. Spec and plan: `archive/specs-and-plans/2026-10-20-github-evidence-spec.md`.

## The tool

`python -m scripts.tools.github_evidence` (token from `GITHUB_TOKEN`, never an option) makes up to two queries of the GitHub commit search per person and guessed year with activity:

1. the person's commits of the year in **all public repositories**: a company address in them is judged like one in the cached clones (personal, project and own domains say nothing, the rest is matched with the register);
2. when no address names an organisation, the person's commits in the **GitHub organisation of an organisation of theirs**: the register gained a `github` column for the login, filled for 55 organisations, each checked against the API.

The attribution rules say that commits in an organisation's own GitHub organisation are evidence of the same kind as a company address, and that the domain of an alliance of companies, such as the BlueDynamics Alliance, says nothing. Responses are cached under `.cache/github-search/`, the pace respects the search limit, and the client now waits out GitHub's secondary rate limit, which stopped the first run after 500 requests. Offline tests with a fake session; docs in the how-to, the attribution rules and the reference page. The migration script, its data and tests moved to `archive/migration-to-registers/`, as planned after #145, so that the register could change without maintaining it.

## The run and the review

1248 searches for 155 people found evidence for 230 runs of years. Reviewed by the rules of #153, applied by script:

- **216 runs accepted** for 143 people. Each becomes a verified row with the evidence as its source; the "not verified" row it cuts is split around it, both parts keeping their note, except the part that starts in 2026, which the current entry verifies. 126 guess rows were cut.
- **4 tie-breaks** by the rule for two records in one year: kitconcept over Pendect (Érico Andrei 2022), RedTurtle over the University of Bologna (Mauro Amico 2021), Reid-McMahon over the Davis Community Network (Steve McMahon 2007 and 2008).
- **2 rejected** as in #153: David Glick's own-company address at kitconcept, Maurits van Rees's old Zest mailbox.
- **1 held** in the proposals file: Jon Pentland, Pretaweb 2017, one commit, while 91 commits of that year carry an iomedia.co.uk address.

Effect on what a trim would cost:

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| guessed person-years with activity | 979 (156 people) | 303 (68 people) |
| share of all commits and pull requests in guessed years | 48% | 15% |

The largest remaining guesses, by activity: Jarn, the University of Jyväskylä, Deloitte Digital, Syslab, Salesforce, Simplon, Eau de Web, kitconcept, Py76. Those are the people who commit with personal addresses at organisations without a GitHub organisation, or whose organisation's repositories they don't commit to.
