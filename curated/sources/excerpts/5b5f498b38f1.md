Jeff Bowman

---

Jeff keeps his fingers in a lot of pies at The Mountaineers. He's been on staff since February 2000 and a member since May 1994. From April 2013 to launch on May 5, 2014, he managed the implementation of an entirely new mountaineers.org website, with Plone and Salesforce as the new CMS and CRM platform. Jeff is also heavily involved with The Mountaineers as a volunteer, having taught and helped design or revise nearly all of the climbing courses they offer. And as if that wasn't enough, Jeff was also the illustrations coordinator and chapter author for the 7th and 8th editions of Mountaineering: The Freedom of the Hills.
