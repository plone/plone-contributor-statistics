Olivier Vergeynst is the Director of the Belgian Institute for Sustainable IT (ISIT-BE). This growing international community of almost 200 members brings together economic actors in favour of a more responsible, ethical, human and environmentally friendly digital transformation. Large companies, SMEs, associations of all sizes, public services and universities benefit from the tools, training and services of the association and contribute to creating new ones. With 20+ years’ experience in international companies as IT Director, Olivier previously led technical teams in Europe, USA and Asia in financial and payment systems’ telecommunications, as well as in IT operations, infrastructure and application development.
