Title: Let a dated record outweigh an address that outlived the job
Author: jensens
Date: 2026-10-21T13:00:50Z

Refs #139. Jens's point about Johannes Raggam: he kept his adm mailbox after he had started his own business, programmatic, and worked for several clients through it, so the adm address on his commits until 2015 says nothing about those years. The record for programmatic is dated: the BlueDynamics Alliance's member page for programmatic was created by him on 19 February 2012 and says "seit Ende 2011 Mitglied"; the archive holds it from November 2013 and February 2014 ([capture](http://web.archive.org/web/20131127152119/http://bluedynamics.com/companies/programmatic/)), and programmatic.pro redirected to it from February 2012. His own site covers 2016 to 2018 (#163).

**Rule.** The attribution rules now say what Maurits van Rees's Zest mailbox had already decided in practice (#153): an address can outlive the employment; where a dated record names another organisation for the same years, the record counts and the address only documents the mailbox. The tie-breaker by commit counts stays for two records of the same kind.

**Data.** programmatic 2012–2018 in one row with the alliance page and his site as sources; adm 2010–2011, with a note on the old mailbox; adm 2008–2009 and Syslab from 2019 unchanged. Derived data and reports regenerated. Once #162 is merged, the adm years 2012–2015 get a row in `rejected_proposals.csv` so the address tool doesn't propose them again.
