Alexander Limi is one of the original Plone founders, and is responsible for its user interface and future directions – as well as guiding the Plone community. He currently serves as vice president on the Plone Foundation board, and works at Plone Solutions in Norway as their Chief Architect and Interaction Designer. His educational background is in Psychology and Computer Science. When he doesn't work on Plone, he enjoys music, plays guitar and spends time with his friends across the globe.
