Title: Map 30 contributors by the public records of their organisations
Author: jensens
Date: 2026-10-18T12:32:24Z

Part of #86. Follows #88.

For 30 contributors, public records show a different organisation than `organisations.csv` for some or all of their active years. The records are conference bios, Plone Foundation pages, archived personal and company sites, CVs, package credits, and company addresses in commits.

## How each timeline works

- The most recent documented organisation is the current entry. It gets a row in `organisation_mapping_sources.csv`.
- Earlier documented organisations become overrides, each with its source.
- Years without a record are Unmapped (#84).
