Codigo23 and Pexego are proud to organize a Plone World Day meetup in Galicia. If you will be in Lugo (or nearby Lugo) on Friday, you are welcome to visit us and participate in this event. We will have some talks covering both plone as a content management system (from a user point of view) and a development framework (from a developer point of view). There will be also afterwards time to have some beers and share plone experiences in a nearby club/brewery/café.
