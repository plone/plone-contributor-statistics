Michael McFadden

---

Michael "Flip" McFadden has been a Plonista for 12 years while maintaining Radio Free Asia's Plone installations for all its Asian languages, along with other spin-off sites such as BenarNews.org. He has authored and maintained many add-ons for Plone, and has made numerous custom enhancements to Plone installations that are, at best, slightly clever, but mostly simple exercises in over-engineering.
