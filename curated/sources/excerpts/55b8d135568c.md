For the record: kitconcept GmbH (founded 2016-02-04, Amtsgericht Bonn HRB 22064) is the direct legal continuation of my sole proprietorship that I previously ran under timostollenwerk.net. The change in 2016 was a change of legal form only (from sole proprietorship to GmbH) — not a change of business. The office, the team, the clients and the work were the same before and after the change.

This is relevant to how plone-contributor-statistics attributes my contributions before 2016 (currently mapped to "Timo Stollenwerk (independent)") versus after (mapped to "kitconcept GmbH").
