Add `Source` and `Notes`; review every talk against the title and description of its video (downloaded from YouTube) and the event pages.

Rules (Jens Klein):

1. Only people speak; a talk counts for the organisations of its speakers.
2. What the talk itself says about a speaker's organisation comes first: the video's title or description, the event page; otherwise the organisation mapping of the year, citing its record; otherwise Unmapped.
3. A role in the Foundation, or a community such as PloneGov-BR, is not a speaker's organisation.
   A company team ("by CodeSyntax") counts for the company.
4. Érico Andrei has several roles: per talk where the talk names one, otherwise the mapping.
5. Quaive is a product of Cosent; "Plone JP" is Plone User's Group Japan.
6. A full stream does not count when its parts are published.
7. A talk without a named speaker stays Unmapped, with a note on the organisation the file had.
