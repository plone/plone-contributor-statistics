# Plan: talks, podcasts and work attributed to the Foundation (#119, #116)

Decided by Jens Klein in the review.

## World Plone Day talks (#119)

- Only videos of the type `Talk` are talks; a format check keeps the list of types fixed.
- The Plone Newsroom is a podcast, not a talk; its 2025 episode gets its hosts.

## Podcasts and live series

- `YYYY-podcasts.csv`: `Series`, `Host`, `Organisation`, `Episodes`, `Source`, one row per series, host and year.
- All series are recorded: The Plone Podcast (T. Kim Nguyen, produced by Six Feet Up), The Plone Newsroom (Philip Bauer, Fred van Dijk), Plone Ask Me Anything (Astrid Beyers), Plone Hands-On, Mão na Massa (PloneGov-BR), Weekly Volto Live.
- Live series count as "podcast / live event" of the points file.
- A series counts for a year with at least five episodes in that year; its 6 points are split among its hosts.

## Work attributed to the Plone Foundation (#116)

- World Plone Day talks: the speakers' organisations from the mapping, instead of "Plone Foundation".
- Plone Conference 2020 and 2021: the Foundation was the organiser and financial agent; the organisers are the people who did the organising work, through the mapping of the year; small items such as keynote images or the logo don't count; the Namur host team of the planned in-person conference 2020 doesn't count.
- Plone Conference 2024: organised by PloneGov-BR, GruPy-DF and PyLadies-DF; the Foundation, APyB and Simples Consultoria supported it.

## PloneGov-BR and Interlegis

- PloneGov-BR is a community of practice; Interlegis is a programme of the Federal Senate and an employer.
- Events in Brasília organised by PloneGov-BR count for PloneGov-BR; public bodies that host them are venues.
- Interlegis staff count for Interlegis in their personal roles.
