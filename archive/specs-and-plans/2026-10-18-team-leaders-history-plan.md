# Plan: team leaders 2004–2025 with sources

Part of #86 and #72: every row of the team leader files needs a public record, and the files reach back as far as the records do.

## Definition, decided by Jens Klein in the review

- A team leader is someone named as the team's lead in the Board minutes or on the team's page, or who represented the team at the Steering Circle, the meeting of the team leads.
- A team can have several leaders in a year; the recognition points are split among them, as before.
- An appointment by the Board counts until a source records a successor or an end.
  Example: Víctor Fernández de Alba was appointed Frontend (Volto) Team lead in November 2017, and Piero Nicolli is lead since January 2025.
- Security Team: the team page says it has no formal leader, and names Alexander Loechel as its official spokesperson; he counts, and so does Maurits van Rees where he reported for the team at the Steering Circle.
- Not teams in this sense: Board committees (IP, Trademark, License, Fundraising), the Board's own Marketing Chair, Google Summer of Code, Sponsorship, the Release Team (release managers count on their own), PLOG and the Steering Circle itself.
- Not a lead: wording such as "convener" or "unofficial coordinator", and pages that are clearly stale.

## Sources

- The Board minutes, read once into a local copy.
- plone.org team pages and their Wayback copies, the old teams overview of 2004–2008.
- Team reports and Steering Circle minutes on community.plone.org.
- Annual reports and Board nominations.
